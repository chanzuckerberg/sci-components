Drop Shadows

Drop shadows provide depth to designs via elevation to help users distinguish between overlapping layers of content.

Types of Drop Shadows

Visual

Shadow Type

Usage

Large Shadow

Containers that layer on top of all other page content (bottom  panels, side panels, modals, etc.)

Medium Shadow

Containers that are part of a page's content, but appear on user input, layering over primary content (dropdown menus, tooltips, etc.)

Small Shadow

Containers that layer closely over content, including images of documents/screens placed in content, and sign up forms
