Introduction to Cells

Cells are guidelines for flows and functionality that often span multiple pages or screens, combining Bases, Genes, and DNA.

Cells

Flows

Cells are created when multiple Bases, Genes, and DNA are combined to create multi-step and occasionally multi-page flows. They serve as guidelines for designers and engineers to follow and are not self-contained components in and of themselves. Instead, they provide best practices for designers and engineers to consider when implementing similar functionality in their products.
